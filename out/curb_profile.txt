========== ORS 801.368 clause 2, both readings ==========
The statute reads: not more than 18 feet wide at any point between two
intersections. Stages 06 and 08 read that as a maximum. No Oregon
appellate decision, AG opinion or ODOT guidance construing it was found,
so both populations are reported and neither is baked into the export.
